What We Do

Scholars Squad supports students through practical help in three main areas.

Maths Tutorials

Free and affordable maths support for high school and university students. We break down difficult topics and help you build confidence.

High School Guidance

Advice and mentoring for students still in high school , subject choices, study strategies, and preparation for university.

Accommodation Support

Help connecting students with safe and affordable accommodation options near campus or in town.

Membership & Applications

Joining Scholars Squad is simple. Every student who joins becomes a member automatically.

For Students

Once you join, you are a member right away. No long forms or waiting periods , just start receiving support.

  • Automatic membership for every student who joins
  • Access to maths tutorials and guidance
  • Information about accommodation options
